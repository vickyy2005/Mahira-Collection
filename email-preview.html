How this renders

email/order-confirmation.html is the master template. Everything between <!-- ITEM:START --> and <!-- ITEM:END --> is repeated once per line item; every other {{field}} is a straight substitution. The same merge that runs on this page is what a mail service would run server-side.

Merge fields

base_url, order_id, customer_name, order_date, track_url, subtotal, shipping, total, address_label, address_name, address_line, address_city, address_phone, payment_method, help_url, preferences_url, unsubscribe_url, and per item item_image, item_title, item_variant, item_qty, item_line_total.

Why it is built this way

Tables and inline styles, because Gmail and Outlook strip most of a stylesheet. Georgia stands in for Playfair and Helvetica for Montserrat, since custom fonts do not load in most clients. The tick and the closing rule are drawn with characters and table cells rather than images, so the message still reads when a client blocks images — which most do by default. The button carries a VML fallback so Outlook renders it as a real button rather than a bare link.
